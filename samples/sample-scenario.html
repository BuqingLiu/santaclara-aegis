1 · Scenario Description

Setup: Ego at 50 km/h on a 4-lane urban arterial. A pedestrian steps into the crosswalk from behind a parked delivery van (occluded until t = 4.0 s). The pedestrian is within 24 m at first sight.

Expected behavior: AEB engages at t = 6.0 s after TTC drops below 1.5 s; full stop achieved by t = 8.0 s. No contact.

Regulatory reference: CA DMV Autonomous Vehicle Program — VRU Interaction (Pedestrian).

4 · Reproducible script (Python · CARLA 0.9.16)

import carla, random

client = carla.Client('localhost', 2000)
client.set_timeout(10.0)
world  = client.load_world('Town03')

# Deterministic seed
random.seed(42)
world.weather = carla.WeatherParameters.ClearNoon

# Spawn ego at known transform
ego_tf = world.get_map().get_spawn_points()[42]
ego = world.spawn_actor(world.get_blueprint_library().filter('vehicle.tesla.model3')[0], ego_tf)

# Trigger pedestrian at t = 4.0 s, 24 m ahead
ped_tf = carla.Transform(ego.get_location() + carla.Location(x=24))
ped = world.spawn_actor(world.get_blueprint_library().filter('walker.pedestrian.*')[0], ped_tf)

# Run for 10 s at 20 Hz, log to telemetry.csv
spectator = world.get_spectator()
spectator.set_transform(carla.Transform(ego.get_location() + carla.Location(z=50),
                                         carla.Rotation(pitch=-90)))
while True:
    world.tick()
